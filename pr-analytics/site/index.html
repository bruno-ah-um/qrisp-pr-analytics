Metric definitions & limitations

  • Pending requests: individual reviewers who are currently requested on a non-draft PR and have not reviewed since. Once they submit a review, GitHub removes the request, unless it is made again. Team requests are shown in the PR table but are not expanded into individual team members.
  • First review time: time from PR creation to the first submitted review by a human other than the PR author. Bot reviews (e.g. CodeRabbit) are ignored. The median covers every PR, open, merged or closed, whose first review happened in the last 30 days. This is not necessarily the time from review-request creation.
  • Reviewer backlog: counts currently outstanding requests, not all PRs a person could optionally review.
  • History: snapshots are collected four times daily. Historical charts begin filling after the workflow is enabled.
  • Visibility: this Pages site and its JSON data are public. Only publish information you are comfortable exposing.
